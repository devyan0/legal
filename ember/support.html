A link won't save

Ember fetches videos directly from Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok. A save can fail for reasons outside the app: the account is private, the post was deleted, the video is age-restricted, or the platform changed how its pages work. Tap Retry on the failed item first. If it keeps failing, send us the link — that is the fastest way for us to fix a broken extractor.

Help us investigate

Please include your device model, iOS version, Ember app version (Settings → Version), which platform the video came from, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot is helpful when available. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.
