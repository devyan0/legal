Ember — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Overview

Ember is designed to work without an account. We collect the minimum data needed to run the service, and we never sell your data.

Data we collect

  • Videos you save stay on your device — the video files, transcripts, categories, and source links Ember stores are kept in local storage on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us and we cannot read them.
  • Transcription and categorization run on-device — the speech in your saved videos is transcribed on your iPhone. Transcript text is never sent to us or to an AI provider.
  • App and device identifiers — a random identifier generated by the app, plus app version, language, and operating-system version. It is not your Apple ID and does not identify you across other companies' apps.
  • Anonymous usage events — basic events such as app opens, a save completing, a playback starting, or a search being run. We record the length of a search query, never the query text itself, and never the caption, transcript, or link of any video you saved.
  • Requests to the original platform — when you save a link, Ember contacts Instagram, YouTube, or TikTok directly from your device to fetch the video and its metadata. Those companies see that request under their own privacy policies. We do not proxy it and we do not log it.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up is required; we do not collect your name, email, or contacts.
  • No advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive anonymized purchase state (e.g. whether a subscription is active) to unlock features, but we never see your payment details.

How expiry works

Ember hides saved videos you haven't watched after a set period. To be clear about what that does and does not delete:

  • Expiring is not deleting. An expired video moves into your Expired list. It stays on your device and you can restore it at any time.
  • Later, only the video file is cleared to free up storage. The transcript, categories, and original link remain, so the video still turns up in search and can be fetched again from the source.
  • Nothing is erased on a timer. Your records are removed only when you delete them yourself, or when you delete the app.

Data retention & deletion

Everything you save lives on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. You can also delete individual videos, or use Privacy → Delete all saved videos, at any time. Anonymous usage events are tied to a random device identifier, not to your identity; that identifier is removed when you delete the app. You can export everything you saved as a JSON file from Privacy → Export my data.
