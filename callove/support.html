Frequently asked questions

How do I manage or cancel my Plus subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Callove. You can change between monthly and yearly or cancel there. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Plus stays active until the end of the current period. Callove also shows a shortcut to this screen in the "Me" tab.

I paid but Plus or my sparks are missing.

Open the "Me" tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. If it still does not appear after a few minutes, email us with the receipt from Apple and the date of purchase.

What are sparks?

Sparks are an in-app currency for extra advanced chat turns and photos beyond your free or Plus allowance. You buy them through Apple. The current daily check-in reward grants chat allowances rather than sparks. Sparks never expire. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred. Your "Me" tab shows a ledger of every reward, purchase, and use.

Can I get a refund?

All purchases go through Apple, so refunds follow Apple's policy. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple determines refund eligibility; mandatory consumer rights remain applicable. If a chat reply or photo fails on our side, the resource is refunded to your ledger automatically.

A photo failed or never arrived.

Photos are generated in the background, so you can leave the chat and come back. If the job fails on our side, the sparks or photo allowance are returned automatically and a refund row appears in your ledger. If a photo has been pending for more than an hour, email us with the approximate time you requested it.

How do I see, edit, or delete what the character remembers?

Open the Character tab, then Profile, then Memory. Each card shows what was remembered, the message it came from, and when. You can edit or delete any card, or delete them all. Deleted memory content is erased immediately and is no longer used in future replies. A deletion marker containing the record ID and deletion time remains to synchronize the change across your devices. You can export your memories, diaries, and conversations as JSON or text from Settings.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings and tap Delete Account. Your profile, conversations, memories, diaries, and photos are deleted without a 30-day grace period. Purchase records are kept for as long as needed to prevent duplicate credits, restore eligible subscriptions, handle refunds or disputes, and meet applicable legal retention requirements. See our Privacy Policy for the retention details. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings as described above.

How do notifications work?

With notification permission, the current app can schedule an on-device reminder using available character content and your selected time. This is a local reminder, not a new server-generated message delivered while the app is closed. You can change the window or turn the message off there, or disable Callove notifications entirely in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications.

Is the character a real person?

No. Every character is an AI, and every photo is generated. During role-play the character may stay in character, but if you ask directly whether it is an AI it will tell you the truth. It cannot meet you or contact you outside the app.

How do I report a message?

Long-press any message and choose Report, then pick a reason. We review reports and may remove content or restrict accounts. See the Terms of Service for what is and is not allowed.
