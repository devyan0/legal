Frequently asked questions

How do I manage or cancel my Plus subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through the store you subscribed in. On iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Callove. On Android, open the Google Play app, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, and select Callove. You can change between monthly and yearly or cancel there. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Plus stays active until the end of the current period. Callove also shows a shortcut to this screen in the "Me" tab.

I paid but Plus or my sparks are missing.

On iPhone, open the "Me" tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. On Android, make sure Google Play on the device uses the Google account you bought with, then reopen Callove; the app checks your Google Play purchases again each time it opens. If it still does not appear after a few minutes, email us with the receipt from Apple or the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and the date of purchase.

What are sparks?

Sparks are an in-app currency for extra advanced chat turns and photos beyond your free or Plus allowance. You buy them through the App Store or Google Play. The current daily check-in reward grants chat allowances rather than sparks. Sparks never expire. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred. Your "Me" tab shows a ledger of every reward, purchase, and use.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases go through the store you bought from, so refunds follow that store's policy. For App Store purchases, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play purchases, see Google Play's refund policy. The store determines refund eligibility; mandatory consumer rights remain applicable. If a chat reply or photo fails on our side, the resource is refunded to your ledger automatically.

A photo failed or never arrived.

Photos are generated in the background, so you can leave the chat and come back. If the job fails on our side, the sparks or photo allowance are returned automatically and a refund row appears in your ledger. If a photo has been pending for more than an hour, email us with the approximate time you requested it.

How do I see, edit, or delete what the character remembers?

Open the Character tab, then Profile, then Memory. Each card shows what was remembered, the message it came from, and when. You can edit or delete any card, or delete them all. Deleted memory content is erased immediately and is no longer used in future replies. A deletion marker containing the record ID and deletion time remains to synchronize the change across your devices. You can export your memories, diaries, and conversations as JSON or text from Settings.

How do I keep my account on a new phone, or move between iPhone and Android?

Link a sign-in method in Callove Settings: Apple or Google on iPhone, Google on Android. On the new device, choose to restore your account and sign in with the same Apple or Google account. Android offers Google sign-in only, so if your account is linked only to Apple, link Google on your iPhone first. One Callove account can be linked to both Apple and Google, and either one opens the same account. Linking never merges two Callove accounts. You can unlink a sign-in method while another one remains.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings and tap Delete Account, or use the account deletion page if you cannot open the app. Your profile, conversations, memories, diaries, and photos are deleted without a 30-day grace period. Purchase records are kept for as long as needed to prevent duplicate credits, restore eligible subscriptions, handle refunds or disputes, and meet applicable legal retention requirements. See our Privacy Policy for the retention details. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play as described above.

How do notifications work?

With notification permission, the app can schedule an on-device reminder using available character content and your selected time. This is a local reminder, not a new server-generated message delivered while the app is closed. You can change the window or turn the message off there, or disable Callove notifications entirely in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications.

App versions with push notifications, including the Android app, can also receive notifications from the Callove server through Firebase Cloud Messaging (on iPhone, delivered through Apple's push service): a new message from a character, with the character's name and a short preview; a diary that is ready; or a character starting a conversation. To stop them, turn notifications off in Callove Settings, in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications, or on Android in Settings › Apps › Callove › Notifications. When you turn notifications off in Callove or sign out, the device's push token is removed from our server.

Is the character a real person?

No. Every character is an AI, and every photo is generated. During role-play the character may stay in character, but if you ask directly whether it is an AI it will tell you the truth. It cannot meet you or contact you outside the app.

How do I report a message?

Long-press any message and choose Report, then pick a reason. We review reports and may remove content or restrict accounts. See the Terms of Service for what is and is not allowed.
