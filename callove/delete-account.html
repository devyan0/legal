Delete your Callove account

Developer: devyan0 · Last updated: October 14, 2026

You can delete your Callove account and its data at any time, either inside the app or by sending us a request from this page. You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion.

Option 1 — In the app

Open Callove, go to Me → Settings → Delete Account, and confirm. Deletion is immediate.

Option 2 — Request by email

Send an email from the address you want us to reply to. Include your Callove account ID (Me → Settings → About shows it) or, if you cannot open the app, the approximate date you started using Callove and the store you installed from (App Store or Google Play).

Request account deletion

Email: haeundae96@gmail.com. We confirm by reply and complete deletion within 7 days of verifying the request.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, characters, conversations, memories, diaries, generated photos and any photos you sent, notification settings, and device push tokens.
  • The link between your Callove account and your Apple or Google account.
  • Analytics data tied to your account identifier (deletion is requested from our analytics provider at the same time).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (product, store transaction identifier, date, amount) for as long as needed to prevent duplicate credits, restore eligible subscriptions, handle refunds or disputes, and meet legal retention requirements.
  • Operational log lines with a shortened, pseudonymous account identifier, which expire 90 days after they were written.

Before you delete

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it first in your Apple ID subscriptions or in the Google Play subscriptions center. Deletion is permanent; there is no recovery period. You can export your data first from Settings → Export.
